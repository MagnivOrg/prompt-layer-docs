Share a request log with teammates or with people outside your workspace.

## Share with your workspace

Anyone in the same workspace can open a request log from its URL. Open the request and copy the address from your browser.

## Share a public link

1. Open the request in **Requests**.
2. Click **⋮** in the top right of the Request Log, then **Share Settings**.
3. Turn on **Public link** and click **Copy link**.

Anyone with the link can view the request. Turn **Public link** off to stop sharing.
